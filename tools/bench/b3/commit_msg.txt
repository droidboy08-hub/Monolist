Play YouTube's Opus stream whenever it is offered, and log the format chosen

The app played whichever audio format /player listed with the highest peak
bitrate, whatever its codec. When AAC 140's peak came out a little above
Opus 251's, a song that YouTube offers as 48 kHz Opus played as 44.1 kHz
AAC, and mpv had to resample it on the way out. IPeJ7iM55hc was one:
131,770 against 130,713 b/s. Nothing in the log said which format played or
why, and a dynamic-range-compressed copy of a format would have been taken
like any other.

- The choice is now an order: Opus 774, Opus 251, AAC 141, AAC 140, then
  the rest by bitrate. A DRC copy (isDrc, or "drc" in its xtags) is passed
  over while any other format is offered. The video's own sound comes
  before a dubbed track. Nothing depends on the order formats are listed
  in, so a second /player call makes the same choice. Formats offered only
  behind signatureCipher are still skipped, and itag 18 is still the
  fallback when no audio-only format has a plain link.
- Every resolve logs what it chose and from what, with the codec taken
  from the mimeType: "innertube: <id> chose itag 251: opus, 48 kHz,
  125 kbps (of 139 140 249 250 251)". The link itself is never logged.
- The setting youtube.format=bitrate switches back to the old rule.
- --format-test: 21 checks against the stand-in server, on two real
  answers with their links replaced and on invented ones.

Measured on Windows the same day, on the instrumented build: bench_B seq
over the 12 benchmark tracks, 4 runs per arm, with the old rule (the
switch) as the control on the same binary.
- Both rules chose the same itags on these 12 tracks: 251 on 44 of 48
  resolves, and 140 on CmThpha4Hoo, which offers no Opus.
- Outside one process, the only resampled opens were CmThpha4Hoo's (6 of
  72). In that one process googlevideo refused all 12 first links with
  403, CmThpha4Hoo's 140 included, and the itag 18 rescues played as AAC.
- IPeJ7iM55hc: Opus 251 on 4 of 4 opens with no resampling. The old rule
  gave AAC 140 on 4 of 4, all resampled.
- A new track in a warm session, time to audio (median / p90): 483 / 753
  ms without the refused process (n=33), against 474.5 / 804 for the old
  rule (n=44). With it included: 525.5 / 4,030 (n=44).
