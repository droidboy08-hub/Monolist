Ask mpv for the song before writing it down, and say what mpv plays (B7)

Every song start waited for its own bookkeeping before mpv was even
asked for the sound: the status line, the play written to History and
play_events, and Home's Recently played and the History list reloaded
with it. That was 40-60 ms on an empty history, and over half a second
with a few hundred songs in Recently played, because the History table
rebuilds every row on each play (now ROADMAP F35). handleResolved now
loads first and does the rest after, and a play is written down only if
mpv took the load; local files and plain URLs do the same. Closing the
song being left is measured at once and written on the next turn of the
event loop (at once on quit).

Now Playing's "SOUND: INNERTUBE" note becomes one line built from what
mpv reports once the sound has started (Player.streamInfo, ROADMAP P06's
engine half): "Streaming · InnerTube · Opus · 48 kHz · 133 kbps", with
"44.1 → 48 kHz" when the device's rate differs. Each file start writes
the long form to the log as a stream: line: codec, the decoder's and the
device's rate, channels and format, the bitrate (the file's average when
it holds sound alone, else what its container declares, else mpv's
measure), resampling and mpv's volume. The unused
demuxer-readahead-secs=20 is gone: with the cache on, mpv ignores it.

Measured with the instrumented Release build, bench_B seq --again and
skip over the 12 ids, two rotations, before and after interleaved
(median / p90; new n=22, replay n=24, skip n=22):
- Empty history: resolved to loadfile 40/59, 61.5/68, 45.5/58 ms -> 0/1,
  1/1, 1/1. TTFA 378.5 -> 324 (new), 286.5 -> 259 (replay),
  263 -> 247.5 (skip): replay and skip fell less than the time saved.
- 480 songs in Recently played: resolved to loadfile 540.5, 535, 535.5 ms
  -> 0, 1, 1. TTFA 839 -> 320, 803 -> 178.5, 812 -> 173 ms.
- Rows written (recent, plays, play_events, closed, labelled): the same
  in all 16 runs.

Checked: Debug build clean (qmllint), --listen-test 35/0,
--scrobble-test 32/0, --queue-test; the stream line matches mpv -v's
AO and swresample lines for an Opus and an AAC stream, JioSaavn, and
local Opus, AAC-with-cover and video files.
