MONOLIST.

A music player for your desktop

Monolist is a free, open-source music player for Windows and macOS. Search and play music from YouTube Music and JioSaavn, keep your own playlists and liked songs, import playlists from Spotify and other services, and listen with lyrics, sound effects and an equaliser.

Download for Windows

Your library is yours

Monolist has no servers and no accounts. Your library stays on your computer. If you want it on another computer too, you can sign in with Google and Monolist keeps a backup in a private folder in your own Google Drive, which only Monolist and you can reach. Read the privacy policy.

Open source

The code is on GitHub, where you can also report a problem.
