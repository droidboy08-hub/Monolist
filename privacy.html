MONOLIST.

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Monolist is a free, open-source music player for Windows and macOS. It has no servers and no accounts of its own. We, the people who make Monolist, never receive your library, your listening or anything about you.

What stays on your computer

Your library lives on your computer: your playlists, the songs you like, what you have played and your settings. Monolist keeps it in its own data folder and nowhere else, unless you turn on one of the options below.

Back up and sync with Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google in Settings (on Windows; it is not yet available on macOS), Monolist asks Google for two things only:

  • Its own hidden folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Monolist keeps one backup file there, so your library follows you to another computer. It cannot see, open or change any of your other files in Drive.
  • Your email address, to show you which Google account the backup is in.

The backup file holds your playlists and their songs, the songs you like, the albums and playlists you saved, what you have played and the songs you turned down, and what you removed from these (so that it is removed on your other computers too), each with when. It holds no downloads or file paths, no settings, no other account's sign-in, and nothing that names your computer. Export in Settings saves the same file wherever you choose.

The backup file stays in your Google account. It goes straight between your computer and Google; it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Monolist keeps its sign-in to Google encrypted for your Windows account (with Windows' data protection, DPAPI) in a file in Monolist's own data folder on your computer, never in its music database or any log; Sign out deletes it.

To delete the backup: in Google Drive, open Settings > Manage apps > Monolist > Options > Delete hidden app data. To take away Monolist's access: myaccount.google.com/permissions, or Sign out in Monolist's Settings.

Monolist's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is used only to keep your own backup, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a person.

The services Monolist talks to

To find and play music, Monolist connects to these services directly from your computer, signed out, the way a web browser would:

  • YouTube Music and YouTube, to search and to play. If you import a YouTube Music sign-in, it is used to read your own library and, if you switch it on, to add what you play to your YouTube history; on Windows it is kept encrypted in Monolist's data folder, as the Google Drive sign-in is.
  • JioSaavn, to search and to play songs from it.
  • LRCLIB, for lyrics.
  • Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, JioSaavn and YouTube pages, only when you import a playlist from a link you paste, to read that playlist's songs.
  • Last.fm, only if you connect it, to record what you play on your Last.fm profile.
  • GitHub, to check for a newer version of Monolist.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy, which applies to what you do with it.

Children

Monolist is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing about anyone.
